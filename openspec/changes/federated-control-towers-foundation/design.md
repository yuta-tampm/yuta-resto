## Context

Gate 1 và Gate 2 đã duyệt capability `tooling/federated-control-tower-transport` riêng với Bridge v1. Delta Spec có 14 Requirements / 39 Scenarios. Bridge v1 hiện là một gateway, Browser QA vẫn PAUSED và `BLOCKED_BY_ENVIRONMENT` (16 PASS, 6 PARTIAL_EVIDENCE, 2 NOT_RUN), Gate 3 `NOT_READY`. Không có implementation federation hoặc bằng chứng live federation. Design này mô tả contract cho một executor Codex cục bộ và các conversation được Human chọn; nó không kích hoạt mode, tạo record vận hành, sửa prompt/skill hoặc chạy QA.

## Goals / Non-Goals

- Cho `PAGE_LOCAL` dùng đúng `PAGE_CONTROL_TOWER` đã active, và chuyển `CROSS_MODULE`/`UNCERTAIN` tới `GLOBAL_CONTROL_TOWER`, trong khi Product/shaping authority của owning Page Chat và Human Gates giữ nguyên Workflow v3.
- Ràng buộc role/scope/instance, chứng minh một executable active target trong một execution context cục bộ, fence lệnh cũ, duy trì at-most-once/lineage/budgets và handoff có provenance ngoài lịch sử chat.
- Giữ Bridge v1 là mode mặc định độc lập; một thay đổi mode cần authorization riêng. Browser QA federation phải chứng minh runtime, không kế thừa PASS của Bridge v1.
- Không đổi Workflow v3, Page Chat rules, Product code/UI, API, auth, schema, provider, deployment, Bridge v1 owner hoặc QA. Không export transcript, lưu credential/secret, thêm executable protocol block, tự cấp side-effect authority hay hỗ trợ đồng thời nhiều host.

## Decisions

### D1. Mode, tower identity và target binding

`EXECUTION_CONTEXT_ID` là uppercase token duy nhất được tạo một lần cho công việc đã được Human/workflow xác định; nó không phải `RUN_ID` và không tái dùng. Context mới ở mode `BRIDGE_V1`. Federation chỉ được chọn qua current-user decision hoặc workflow đã review gắn **context, một Windows host, canonical checkout root và scope**; thiếu hoặc mâu thuẫn binding thì không activation. Một context chỉ có một activation record. Đây là giới hạn vận hành, không phải chứng minh loại trừ đồng thời xuyên máy.

Logical `TOWER_ID` là canonical tuple `(CONTROL_TOWER_ROLE, CONTROL_TOWER_SCOPE, OWNING_PAGE_CHAT_ID | NONE)`. `CONTROL_TOWER_ROLE` chỉ nhận `PAGE_CONTROL_TOWER` hoặc `GLOBAL_CONTROL_TOWER`. Scope Page là exact page/capability owner `PAGE_LOCAL`; Global là `CROSS_MODULE`, `UNCERTAIN`, cross-page, shared/foundation. `OWNING_PAGE_CHAT_ID` bắt buộc cho Page, `NONE` cho Global. Runtime `CONTROL_TOWER_INSTANCE` là exact ChatGPT conversation ID trong Project ID đã chọn; runtime tower identity là `(TOWER_ID, PROJECT_ID, CONTROL_TOWER_INSTANCE)`. Title được bind và đối chiếu trước send nhưng không phải identity hoặc authority. Ví dụ Page/POINTAGE/instance-01 và Page/POINTAGE/instance-02 có cùng logical `TOWER_ID` nếu cùng owner, nhưng là hai runtime identities khác nhau; Global #1→#2 tương tự. Cùng title hoặc tab không chuyển quyền.

Page activation cần current-user hoặc workflow-approved selection nêu exact owning Page Chat/conversation, page scope, Product/shaping provenance và impact classification; Codex đối chiếu Page Chat owner với approved page context, không tự chỉ định owner. Global chỉ điều phối `CROSS_MODULE`/`UNCERTAIN`/shared/foundation và không thay Product decision của Page. Codex kiểm exact visible title, Project ID và URL/conversation ID trước **mỗi** send; sai/mơ hồ thì không gửi. Phương án suy role từ title, tự chọn Page Chat, coi một host name hoặc một chat message là authority bị loại. (F1–F3, F13)

### D2. Single-host lock và local state owner

Future tracked owners remain `.agents/skills/yuta-federated-control-towers/SKILL.md`, `.agents/skills/yuta-federated-control-towers/scripts/state-helper.ps1` and `docs/chatGPT/YUTA_FEDERATED_CONTROL_TOWERS_OPERATING_PROTOCOL.md`. Human applies the latter only to exact selected Page/Global conversation operating contexts; it references Workflow v3 and does not edit Page Chat prompt, global Project Instructions or Bridge v1 owners. No owner is implemented by this Design.

**Supported boundary:** one local Windows host, one canonical checkout on a local NTFS volume, and every Codex executor for this context using the same long-lived helper and context directory. The ignored local directory is `tmp/yuta-federated-control-towers/<EXECUTION_CONTEXT_ID>/` under that checkout. Its exact lock path is `.../lock`; `activation.json`, immutable `handoffs/<HANDOFF_ID>/handoff.json` and immutable `journal/<REVISION>.json` share this directory. The approved context binding includes canonical absolute checkout root and a non-secret host label; process ID, process start UTC and random helper invocation ID are diagnostic owner metadata. They are not cross-host fencing credentials.

The proposed Windows primitive is an open `System.IO.FileStream(lockPath, FileMode.OpenOrCreate, FileAccess.ReadWrite, FileShare.None)` held by the helper for the **entire** command or activation transaction, including browser observation and durable outcome. Acquisition is non-blocking or bounded; a sharing violation means another handle owns it, so no competing execution or forced takeover. The helper releases it by disposing the stream only after outcome/stop state is durable. Windows closes file handles when their process terminates; the `lock` file itself remains and **its presence is not ownership**. A later helper may acquire the released handle but must reconcile journal, activation and pending execution before any new work. Owner metadata with an old PID/start time is stale diagnostic evidence, never a reason to delete a live lock. Never manually delete or replace `lock`; if it cannot be acquired, path identity is uncertain, or owner/process status conflicts, return `BLOCKED`/`HUMAN_REQUIRED`. No stale-lock timeout or PID reuse grants authority.

Two helpers on the same host and **same checkout/context path** contend on the same NTFS file; only the handle holder can transition state. A different checkout has a different lock path and is **unsupported** for the same execution context: its checkout-root binding must mismatch the approved context and activation is refused; if a shared canonical binding cannot be established, stop. A different host is unsupported because its filesystem lock is independent; neither host can claim distributed exclusivity. A copied record, matching title, host label or Human assertion cannot prove cross-host atomicity. If alternate checkout/host execution is known or suspected, stop both dependent flows pending a separately reviewed distributed mechanism. No automatic cross-checkout/multi-host recovery is promised.

Windows sharing semantics and automatic handle close are grounded in Microsoft [CreateFile sharing](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/win32/api/fileapi/nf-fileapi-createfilea) and [closing files](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/win32/fileio/closing-and-deleting-files) documentation. This is a **design selection**, not runtime proof: implementation and QA must verify NTFS path, handle lifetime, write durability and competing-process behavior. If the helper cannot retain the handle across a browser operation, or any executor bypasses it, federation remains non-executable. Chat history, Markdown, an in-memory flag or a timestamp alone cannot substitute for this lock. (F4, F5, F7, F9, F11)

### D3. Exact activation record, epochs và authority transfer

`activation.json` là **một** current-state projection cho context dưới lock, không phải Product/approval source. Mọi key dưới đây tồn tại trong mỗi revision; `null` chỉ được dùng ở trạng thái được nêu. JSON dùng UTF-8, canonical serialization với property names sorted, không có duplicate keys; `RECORD_HASH` là SHA-256 của canonical payload trừ chính `RECORD_HASH`. Unknown schema/version/key, malformed data, hash mismatch hoặc broken previous-revision chain làm context `BLOCKED`, không tự sửa.

Every key in the table is required. `TOWER_ID` is the canonical tuple serialized as structured role/scope/owner fields, never a title-derived free-text token. **D3 budget-schema clarification for Phase 3 review.** This is the local representation of the existing Control Tower/workflow anti-loop ledger, not a new retry authority. Both budget objects are required in every activation snapshot and handoff. Their only domain is `(EXECUTION_CONTEXT_ID, CAUSAL_LINEAGE_ID)`; `RUN_ID`, `ACTIVATION_EPOCH`, role, tower instance, title and display-stage name are occurrence/binding metadata, never fresh budget domains. The enclosing activation/handoff supplies `EXECUTION_CONTEXT_ID`; each budget's `CAUSAL_LINEAGE_ID` must equal the enclosing non-`NONE` lineage. Before a lineage exists, the sole zero form is `RECOVERY_BUDGET = {"CAUSAL_LINEAGE_ID":"NONE","ATTEMPTS_USED":0,"APPROVED_MAXIMUM":0,"EVIDENCE_REFERENCES":[]}` and `EVALUATOR_BUDGET = {"CAUSAL_LINEAGE_ID":"NONE","BUCKETS":[]}`. No active run or handoff may use this zero form. These are exact JSON object shapes: every listed key is required, no unknown or duplicate key is permitted, no `null` replaces a value, integers are non-negative JSON integers (not strings, floats or booleans), and arrays are ordered. Canonical serialization uses the D3 sorted-property UTF-8 JSON and exact case-sensitive strings; array order is chronological for evidence and ascending by `BUCKET_KEY` for buckets. Empty `BUCKETS` is valid only before any evaluator execution for this lineage; it does not assert that prior evaluator history is absent when an earlier canonical ledger shows otherwise.

`BUCKET_KEY` is fixed at first classification and is not regenerated from a mutable label. A new bucket requires evidence of a **materially different** stage or evaluator purpose under the existing iteration ledger; an alias, stage/chat rename, different wrapper, new `ROUND_ID`, fresh `RUN_ID`, rotation or escalation must reuse the old key. If equivalence or lineage ancestry is uncertain, no bucket is created and execution stops for review. A new bucket can have zero used generations; the array remains sorted by `BUCKET_KEY`. A pending command can appear in at most one bucket and must match the exact current command identity and `COMMAND_ACCEPTED` journal intent. Any `PENDING_COMMAND_ID` blocks another equivalent execution until an attributable outcome or Human/workflow reconciliation resolves it. No evidence reference may contain a transcript, customer data, credential, token, cookie or session.

For this local schema, uppercase identity tokens (`CAUSAL_LINEAGE_ID`, `BUCKET_KEY`, `STAGE_KEY`, `PURPOSE_KEY`) match `[A-Z][A-Z0-9_-]*`; only the explicit pre-lineage sentinel is `NONE`. The pair `(STAGE_KEY, PURPOSE_KEY)` and `BUCKET_KEY` are each unique within one lineage. Counted evidence references use `JOURNAL:<non-negative decimal REVISION>:<EVENT_ID>` with no leading zero except `0`; the referenced immutable event must exist in the same context and prove the corresponding execution or failed recovery observation, and its hash chain must verify. This preassigned event ID avoids a self-referential event-hash cycle. `MATERIAL_EQUIVALENCE_REFERENCE` uses `REVIEW:<repository-relative-path>:<lowercase SHA-256>` and must resolve to the reviewed classification/rationale; a path cannot escape the checkout or contain private content. A current Human exception above default is proven by the exact accepted-result, approval, semantic-decision and consumed-proof chain below; generic `APPROVAL_REFERENCES` alone is insufficient. Unresolvable or conflicting references block; a bare counter or label is not proof.

**Evaluator bucket provenance (current authority).** `MATERIAL_EQUIVALENCE_REFERENCE` resolves to the reviewed immutable classification and its approved one-way descriptor/result/approval/semantic record chain below. `APPROVAL_REFERENCES` retains its six-field generic provenance shape and is never a typed classification or approval record. Human or existing reviewed workflow decides SAME/DISTINCT material identity; helper verifies exact scope, bytes, hashes and consumption but cannot decide material difference.

Within one `(EXECUTION_CONTEXT_ID, CAUSAL_LINEAGE_ID)`, materially equivalent work defaults to the existing bucket. A `SAME_MATERIAL_BUCKET` record must name that existing `BUCKET_KEY`; it creates no bucket and no new allowance. A `DISTINCT_MATERIAL_BUCKET` record may create one new zero-used bucket only if its reviewed rationale identifies a materially different stage or evaluator purpose, names the prior comparable bucket (or `NONE` for the first), and the decision explicitly authorizes that distinction. If multiple existing buckets could apply and no approved classification selects one, stop rather than choose or mint a bucket. `BUCKET_KEY`, stable `STAGE_KEY`/`PURPOSE_KEY`, first approved `MATERIAL_EQUIVALENCE_REFERENCE` and counted history are immutable after the first charged execution; the approved maximum can only increase through the separately validated positive Human exception. A later reviewed reclassification may govern only future work through a new decision and journal revision; it cannot rewrite prior counts, reset an exhausted bucket or silently move old executions. A new file, hash, path, stage/evaluator display label, wrapper, `RUN_ID`, epoch, tower instance, restart, rotation or escalation is never material distinction by itself. Missing, mutable, stale, unapproved, conflicting or ambiguous record/decision/hash/ancestry yields zero new executable budget and `NEEDS_REVIEW`/`BLOCKED`.

**Positive Human budget exception (current authority).** `BUDGET_MAXIMUM_EXCEPTION` is the exact typed semantic decision in the one-way authority chain below, not an expanded `APPROVAL_REFERENCES` item. `EXCEPTION_ID=DECISION_ID`; `APPLICATION_ID` does not exist. `NEW_APPROVED_MAXIMUM > PREVIOUS_APPROVED_MAXIMUM` and exact positive `ADDED_ALLOWANCE` are mandatory. The resulting maximum, consumed proof and immutable journal event persist under the same lock; no second budget subsystem or Bridge wire field is created.

The accepted Human result is represented by the canonical `RESULT_CORE_V1` and `ACCEPTED_GATE_RESULT_RECORD` below, not by caller metadata. A positive maximum exception is consumed exactly once through `AUTHORITY_CONSUMED`; used counters, lineage, bucket identity, pending command, current freeze and independent authorization stay unchanged merely because the maximum rises.

`APPROVED_MAXIMUM` is monotonic non-decreasing within one exact recovery or evaluator budget scope. After initial commitment it remains unchanged or rises only through the exact positive Human exception; it never decreases. `ATTEMPTS_USED` and `EXECUTION_GENERATIONS_USED` never decrease. A Human Gate without an exact exception leaves maxima unchanged. Preserve the resulting budget, consumed authority proof, complete journal chain, current active freezes and independent `EVIDENCE_STOP_STATE` through restart, fresh run, Page-to-Global handoff and either rotation. Missing, replayed, stale, wrong-scope or conflicting authority or state blocks; an exception never lifts a freeze, clears evidence-stop or authorizes unrelated execution.

**Separate budget execution freeze.** A later stricter Human decision records one typed freeze transition without lowering `APPROVED_MAXIMUM` or used counters and without clearing `EVIDENCE_STOP_STATE`. The exact authoritative freeze scope, proposed payload, ID formula, aliases, persistence and consumption are defined in the Runtime Authority Identity Model below. Reviewed purpose, stop condition, current counter/maximum and current active-freeze target must match the committed snapshot; otherwise a fresh Human decision is required. Timestamps are diagnostic, never authority or automatic expiry.

One budget scope has at most one active freeze. APPLY, LIFT and SUPERSEDE follow the exact `DECISION_ID` aliases and active-target preconditions below. The old record remains immutable; historical lift/supersession is derived from the verified later transition, never edited in place. No freeze ID may be hashed from a later Human result or constructed by a second formula. Distinct decision items are required if a Human Gate includes both a maximum exception and freeze transition.

An ACTIVE freeze prohibits accepting a new affected recovery/evaluator execution even when numerical capacity remains. For used `2` / maximum `5`, a valid freeze leaves `2` / `5` unchanged while blocking further affected execution; it is a stop, not exhaustion. A freeze after a previously accepted command cannot erase or reclassify that command or its later attributable outcome; pending/uncertain execution stays governed by at-most-once and is counted if actual execution is proved. A maximum exception while frozen can raise the exact maximum but leaves the freeze ACTIVE. LIFT or SUPERSEDE requires a separate current Human decision on the exact same scope and active `FREEZE_ID`; it never increases maximum, resets used counters, clears `EVIDENCE_STOP_STATE` or authorizes execution by itself. A Human Gate without an exact freeze transition leaves freeze state unchanged. Missing, malformed, stale, replayed, duplicate, wrong-context/lineage/bucket, hash-mismatched, conflicting, unsupported or journal/snapshot-inconsistent freeze provenance yields zero executable authority for that scope and `NEEDS_REVIEW`/`BLOCKED`.

Before any affected recovery/evaluator execution can be accepted, validate the authoritative `CURRENT_ACTIVE_FREEZES` snapshot against committed semantic-record/proof/journal transition history; an ACTIVE or ambiguous freeze blocks new acceptance. Under the held D2 lock, an evaluator action first commits `COMMAND_ACCEPTED` and the bucket's `PENDING_COMMAND_ID` in the same verified journal/snapshot revision **before** possible dispatch. `COMMAND_EXECUTING` precedes dispatch. Only proof that the equivalent evaluator actually ran increments `EXECUTION_GENERATIONS_USED` once for that command, appends one evidence reference and clears pending with its attributable `COMMAND_OUTCOME` revision. Success and failure both count. If accepted/executing has no attributable outcome, `EXECUTION_UNCERTAIN` and pending remain non-executable; do not count an unproven run as actual, but do not grant a replacement execution or budget reset. Read-only reconciliation may later prove the actual run and account for it exactly once under lock; uncertainty alone cannot authorize retry. An actual corrective action followed by an observation of the **same** causal blocker increments `ATTEMPTS_USED` once with its evidence reference in the attributable outcome/reconciliation revision. A correction that resolves the blocker, ordinary read-only diagnosis without equivalent evaluator execution, rejected preflight, malformed/stale/duplicate/replayed/unauthorized command, Human Gate round, timeout, reload or result-delivery uncertainty adds no recovery attempt; none of these adds an execution generation unless an actual evaluator execution is independently proved. A read-only observation that actually reruns the equivalent evaluator does consume one generation. One observation may consume one generation and one failed recovery attempt. `BLOCKED`, `STOP` and `DONE` do not increment by themselves; the earlier attributable actual execution remains counted. Budget exhaustion, unsafe retry, missing mandatory evidence or evidence without useful progress stops at the existing affected gate; `EVIDENCE_STOP_STATE`/blocker history is carried unchanged across transfer and is not reset by a Human Gate or status label.

Every budget mutation uses the existing D3/D5 journal-first, complete-snapshot, flush, replace and read-back sequence under one lock; a count never changes independently of the command/observation outcome that justifies it. Handoff copies the exact last committed budget objects, positive-exception evidence, exact `CURRENT_ACTIVE_FREEZES` and `CONSUMED_AUTHORITY_PROOFS`, and committed freeze transition history, lineage, blocker ancestry and `EVIDENCE_STOP_STATE`; source and target must match the journal/snapshot and reviewed Tasks iteration ledger. Page→Global, Page→Page, Global→Global and process restart preserve every count, maximum, bucket key, pending intent, evidence reference and freeze/exception provenance. An epoch or tower instance is a fencing binding only; an older epoch/snapshot/handoff cannot overwrite a newer budget revision. Missing budget state, missing evidence, unknown bucket key/field, malformed value, unsupported schema version, hash mismatch, decreasing counter/maximum, bucket-key conflict, conflicting source/target budget, journal/snapshot ahead/behind mismatch, or inconsistent lineage/context fails closed with zero executable authority and `NEEDS_REVIEW`/`BLOCKED`. No automatic repair, migration, reset or invented retry is allowed. The Phase 2 helper's rejection of nonempty buckets remains truthful historical evidence; the helper must be corrected and revalidated under separate authorization before any Phase 3 use of this clarified shape.

The enclosing activation `SCHEMA_VERSION: 1` and handoff `HANDOFF_VERSION: 1` remain unchanged because Phase 2 never accepted a nonempty evaluator bucket; this completes their previously undefined nested v1 shape. A record or handoff with another version is unsupported, and an older v1 record is usable only if every current exact-shape, provenance and journal-consistency check passes. No automatic version migration or grandfathering of an unvalidated nonempty bucket is permitted.

No field is edited in place. Under the held lock, helper writes a complete new revision to a same-directory temporary file, flushes file buffers with `FileStream.Flush(true)`, then performs same-volume `File.Replace` for an existing `activation.json` (or create-new move for genesis), and reads back hash/revision before work. An immutable `journal/<REVISION>.json` carrying event kind, previous hash, new state and identity is durably written first; `activation.json` is committed only when its referenced event exists. Orphan journal entries, orphan temp files, snapshot/event mismatch, partial read or unproven replace/flush semantics cause **no execution** and bounded Human reconciliation. The successful, verified replacement is a process-crash consistency contract on supported local NTFS; it is not a claim of power-loss or distributed durability. `FileStream.Flush(true)` is documented by Microsoft [here](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dotnet/api/system.io.filestream.flush), and `File.Replace` [here](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dotnet/api/system.io.file.replace). Implementation must prove these assumptions; if it cannot, fail closed.

Existing eight-field `CONSUMED_AUTHORITY_PROOF_V1` and `AUTHORITY_CONSUMED` journal envelope remain unchanged.

Under the held D2 exclusive lock, validate the complete chain and exact current source, append exactly one proof in a complete `AUTHORITY_CONSUMED` `STATE_PAYLOAD`, flush/read back the journal, then replace/flush/read back the activation snapshot before any dependent activation. The journal flush commits one-time consumption. This post-state changes only proof and ordinary revision/journal metadata; role, run, state, budgets and freezes remain intact. Consumption itself never grants `ACTIVE`. The one consumed proof may start only its first matching activation-intent sequence for the reviewed target, run, source ancestor and action; it cannot start another sequence. The subsequent D4/D6 transaction fences an old `ACTIVE` tower under the same lock before target `ACTIVATING`, carries exact proofs in handoff, and requires a fresh read-only handshake, valid command, bound result and target evaluation before D3 `ACTIVE_COMMIT`. Human selection and fresh target probe are independent mandatory conditions. Same-role Page/Global rotation, Page-to-Global escalation or a changed target/run each need a new live-selection decision and fresh run; handoff alone grants nothing. No Page proof can authorize Global, a different Page, context, Project, conversation, instance or run.

After journal flush but before snapshot replacement, restart reconciles that exact committed post-state and never consumes the decision again. Before any activation intent, the same target/run transaction may continue only after exact source, packet, proof, ledger and no-pending-execution checks; it may not choose another target or automatically send a browser command. Once an intent/probe may have occurred, D6 delivery and execution uncertainty rules apply: no automatic resend, replay or `ACTIVE`. Completed, abandoned or terminal activation makes its proof permanently non-reusable. Partial event, stale source, ambiguous authority, conflicting proof, uncertain delivery/execution, probe failure, or absent read-back means zero new executable authority and `BLOCKED`/`NEEDS_REVIEW`. D2 FileShare.None ownership spans this authority-critical browser transaction so another tower cannot become executable in parallel. SelfTest fixture roots cannot supply production-valid approval or live target evidence. Only bounded identity, scope, run, state/epoch and artifact/hash metadata may persist; no transcript, customer data, private Page Chat content, credentials, tokens, cookies or session.

### D4. Immutable handoff contract và consumption

Mỗi transfer có một `HANDOFF_ID` mới (uppercase opaque token) và file immutable `tmp/yuta-federated-control-towers/<EXECUTION_CONTEXT_ID>/handoffs/<HANDOFF_ID>/handoff.json`. Dưới lock, old run phải terminal/revoked và mọi delivery/execution phải có outcome chắc chắn trước khi tạo file. Human/workflow chọn target chính xác và helper **reserve fresh target `RUN_ID` trong terminal record trước khi persist handoff**; ID không được dùng lại nếu transfer hủy. `TARGET_ACTIVATION_EPOCH` trong handoff là expected next epoch (`terminal epoch + 1`), chỉ được gán thật khi `TERMINAL→ACTIVATING` commit và phải so khớp. Vì vậy `TARGET_RUN_ID` là required, không `PENDING` trong foundation này. Handoff không bị overwrite hoặc edit; correction cần `HANDOFF_ID` mới và old handoff được đánh dấu superseded bằng journal event, không xóa lịch sử.

Toàn bộ field sau là **required** ở schema `HANDOFF_VERSION: 1`; `NOT_APPLICABLE` hoặc empty array có lý do được dùng khi một nguồn không áp dụng, không bỏ key. `HANDOFF_HASH` là SHA-256 của canonical UTF-8 JSON trừ chính field hash; unknown/duplicate field, missing key hoặc hash mismatch bị từ chối.

Each tower object uses the D1/D3 required role/scope/owner/Project/conversation fields. `WORKFLOW_STATE` requires change, stage, gate status and source reference. `PRODUCT_PROVENANCE` requires owning Page Chat ID or `NONE`, source references, four-state `PAGE_CONTEXT_INTAKE`, completeness and gaps. `NEXT_CANDIDATE_ACTION` requires action description, expected authority source and `AUTHORIZATION_STATUS: PENDING`; a purported already-approved status is invalid here. All nested approval/hash/budget refs use the D3 shapes. No unreviewed free-text Product content is stored.

Both new handoff arrays participate in the existing canonical `HANDOFF_HASH`. At creation, reconstruct the authoritative source activation state bound by `SOURCE_RECORD_HASH` and copy `CURRENT_ACTIVE_FREEZES` and `CONSUMED_AUTHORITY_PROOFS` exactly. Validate canonical equality of each handoff array against that source state; any already-durable receiving state for the context must equal both arrays exactly before executable activation. No automatic union, larger-set preference, inferred merge, or backfill is permitted. A mismatch is `NEEDS_REVIEW` before activation. Handoff transfers prior consumption but neither creates nor re-consumes semantic authority. Same-role rotation and Page-to-Global handoff use these same rules; fresh `RUN_ID` changes neither array.

Handoff has no time-only expiry; it is stale if source record/epoch/terminal state, target selection, approval reference, artifact hash, budget or context provenance no longer matches canonical evidence. Under lock, persist via create-new temp, `Flush(true)`, same-volume rename to final immutable path and read-back hash; failure leaves transfer quiescent. The next `ACTIVATING→ACTIVE` revision atomically records `CURRENT_HANDOFF_ID`, target run binding and appends the ID to `CONSUMED_HANDOFF_IDS` after recheck. A consumed, superseded, changed or duplicate `HANDOFF_ID` is never accepted again. Crash after writing handoff but before consume leaves it unconsumed **and non-authorizing**; restart requires bounded reconciliation and exact recheck, not automatic activation. Source/target remain non-executable until the D3 active commit.

Handoff summary may be placed in existing v1 multiline fields only after a new run is permitted; no new executable block or wire field is added. `NEXT_CANDIDATE_ACTION` describes an option, while actual next action must be independently authorized from current Human/workflow and approved artifact state. **HANDOFF ≠ AUTHORIZATION** for Product, Human Gate, Apply or any side effect. ### D5. Unchanged v1 wire protocol và durable intent/outcome ledger

Giữ ba block và grammar Bridge v1: `YUTA_BRIDGE_HANDSHAKE`, `YUTA_CODEX_COMMAND`, `YUTA_CODEX_RESULT`, `PROTOCOL_VERSION: 1`, sequential positive `ROUND_ID`, `COMMAND_ID = RUN_ID:ROUND_ID`, exact `CAUSAL_LINEAGE_ID`, one accepted command/one bound result. Không thêm epoch/role/scope vào wire. Verified sender conversation plus durable `RUN_BINDING` resolve epoch and tower (D3) before accepting command; title/stage rename does not change lineage. Result must bind the same run, round, command, lineage, stage and target; result A cannot be reused for B.

`journal/<REVISION>.json` is the immutable local execution-evidence ledger under the same lock, hash chained to the prior event and projected by `activation.json`. Each existing Phase-2 envelope has schema version, revision, event ID, `EVENT_KIND`, context ID, epoch, tower/run/round/command identity or `NOT_APPLICABLE`, previous hash, UTC timestamp, authorization/evidence references and event hash. Existing lifecycle and command event values remain. Every new-model classification, positive maximum exception and freeze transition uses the single `EVENT_KIND: AUTHORITY_CONSUMED` with `STATE_PAYLOAD` as the sole complete intended activation `POST_STATE`, including exactly one new consumed proof and the applicable active-freeze delta specified in D3. No extra journal top-level proof or `POST_STATE` field is admitted. Earlier semantic-specific event-kind names are superseded; they are not alternate valid new-model values. The existing journal-first flush/replace/read-back sequence under the same lock remains mandatory. A durably flushed `AUTHORITY_CONSUMED` event commits consumption even before snapshot replacement; restart reconstructs its exact `STATE_PAYLOAD` and never reapplies the decision. Partial/unverifiable events or journal/snapshot disagreement leave zero executable authority. A duplicate event ID or `(EXECUTION_CONTEXT_ID, RUN_ID, COMMAND_ID)` key creates no new activation or execution. For a command, commit `COMMAND_ACCEPTED` before any side effect, then `COMMAND_EXECUTING` before dispatch, then exactly one attributable `COMMAND_OUTCOME` (`COMPLETED`/`FAILED` with evidence) or unresolved `EXECUTION_UNCERTAIN`. Each transition creates a new immutable journal revision and complete activation snapshot revision; no in-place editing. The ledger is ignored runtime state, not repository approval/Product authority or a new protocol block.

An accepted command without an outcome is treated as **possibly executed** after crash, even if the crash may have preceded dispatch. It is never re-executed merely because browser/result delivery failed. If outcome is durable but result delivery is unknown, keep the command outcome and `DELIVERY_UNCERTAIN` separately; do not resend result without positive proof of non-delivery under Bridge v1 and do not transfer dependent execution. After restart, acquire lock, verify full journal/hash chain and snapshot, then reconcile with read-only repository/browser evidence. Unknown outcome remains `EXECUTION_UNCERTAIN` and needs Human/workflow disposition; Human cannot turn uncertainty into a second execution of the same `COMMAND_ID`. Budget accounting follows actual evaluator execution when provable; unknown execution is reported conservatively and cannot be used to reset a budget.

The six Bridge v1 outbound delivery states remain unchanged. Timeout, reload, missing response, chat reopen or switching tower are not non-delivery proof. Resend is allowed only with positive proof Send never activated or failed before posting, after exact target and lineage recheck; duplicate command execution is never allowed. Prose-only, partial/unknown/duplicate/malformed block, stale round/epoch/run/lineage, wrong target and replay are non-executable. The helper lock is held from preflight through durable outcome/uncertain state; if browser transport cannot operate while it is held, no federation execution. (F8, F9, F13)

### D6. Ordered transfer, rotation và crash recovery

**Page instance rotation:** impact classification remains `PAGE_LOCAL`; bind same owning Page Chat/logical `TOWER_ID`, obtain Human/reviewed workflow selection of different exact conversation instance, stop dependent work, prove no outstanding command/delivery, commit old `FENCING` and `TERMINAL`, create immutable handoff with new reserved `RUN_ID`, recheck approvals/hashes/Page provenance, verify new Project/title/URL/ID and live instructions, commit `ACTIVATING` with new epoch, run only the read-only probe, then commit `ACTIVE`. Old run/epoch commands/results remain rejected. **Global instance rotation** uses the same order but carries CROSS_MODULE/UNCERTAIN coordination scope and all blockers/evaluator budgets; no Page Product authority is added.

**Page→Global escalation:** on impact change, Page tower stops local expansion and dependent execution; `CROSS_MODULE`/`UNCERTAIN` classification and reason are recorded. Human/reviewed workflow selects the exact Global target before handoff; selection does not make it active. While holding lock, close all known command/result delivery with certainty or stop. Commit Page `FENCING` and terminal/revoked run, reserve fresh target run in terminal record, persist handoff with causal root, budgets, approval/hashes and Page context gaps; verify exact Global target, commit Global `ACTIVATING` with the expected epoch, perform read-only probe, commit `ACTIVE`, then accept only separately authorized Global commands. During the gap no tower performs dependent work. After fence, Page may not issue even read-only bridge commands for this context; independent read-only repository diagnosis still needs its own valid authority and may not reopen the old run. Global never creates a missing PAGE_LOCAL Product decision. A stale Page command is rejected by source/epoch/run binding.

Global remains the sole executable tower while that exact selection is reviewed and validated. Under the same D2 `FileShare.None` lock, the transaction consumes one valid typed Human selection proof, closes or fails closed on pending command/outcome/delivery, then commits Global `FENCING` and terminal/revoked state before any Page `ACTIVATING` or executable state. The fencing commit is the transfer linearization point: from then until a separately verified Page `ACTIVE_COMMIT`, neither tower is executable. Only after Global is terminal/revoked may the helper reserve the fresh Page run in the terminal record and persist the D4 immutable, non-authorizing handoff; a proposed handoff before fencing is review context, never a D4 handoff file or authority. Recheck exact target URL/title/Project/conversation, source record and all carried proofs, budgets, freezes, blockers and evidence-stop state before the read-only Page probe and Page `ACTIVE_COMMIT`. Wrong/stale target, conflicting snapshot or journal, uncertain browser delivery/outcome, failed lock/fence/read-back, or incomplete handoff leaves zero executable authority and requires bounded recovery or Human review, never a second active tower or automatic resend. ### D7. Page context, authority và privacy minimization

`PAGE_CONTEXT_INTAKE` always records `AVAILABLE`, `PARTIAL`, `UNKNOWN` or `NOT_APPLICABLE` with owning Page Chat ID, source reference, question scope, completeness and gaps. `AVAILABLE` requires sufficiently identified relevant context from the owning Page tower or a Human-authorized read-only handoff; direct cross-chat retrieval is optional/best effort. `PARTIAL` blocks decisions depending on gaps; `UNKNOWN` never means no prior requirement and may not be replaced by a similar chat. Repository read-only evidence owns current implementation, owning Page Chat owns PAGE_LOCAL Product/shaping intent; discrepancy is reported, not silently resolved. Page role adds no Product/Gate/side-effect authority. Global does not create missing Page decisions. Codex opens a Page Chat only when explicit federated mode, exact active Page tower and Human/workflow target authorization all hold; Bridge v1 still forbids direct Page Chat orchestration.

The ignored runtime records retain **minimum metadata**, not chat content: exact Project/conversation ID and title for target comparison; owning Page Chat ID; bounded decision/evidence references, hashes and completeness/gap labels; no full Page context excerpt unless a later review finds a strictly necessary bounded excerpt and approves its retention. Exact IDs/titles can identify private conversations, so access remains limited to the local checkout and the record lifetime ends when the context is intentionally closed; deletion is a separately authorized cleanup after terminal/verified no pending command, never a way to clear an active lock, replay history or uncertainty. No new legal retention period is invented. Do not persist transcript text, export private chats, store customer personal data, credentials, tokens, cookies, sessions, auth material or unrelated private conversation content. No provider-control API, auth change, new network channel or browser/filesystem permission beyond exact reviewed target access and ignored local record writes is authorized. A need for any of those, cross-host coordination or a new executable wire field returns `NEEDS_REVIEW` before Tasks/Apply. (F2, F3, F11–F13)

- **Sensitive Design Gate: TRIGGERED, NOT_APPROVED.** Human phải duyệt riêng local single-host/one-checkout boundary, exact NTFS file-handle lock, activation/handoff/journal metadata, epoch/fence/linearization, Page/Global direct browser targets, crash fail-closed và 35-case QA obligation. Design approval không tự phê duyệt Sensitive Design Gate.
- Split-brain, stale instance, replay, lost/incomplete/replayed handoff, giả approval, budget reset, scope escalation, partial write và crash sau side effect còn là rủi ro phải chứng minh bằng implementation/QA. File lock/epoch/journal là contract, chưa phải PASS. ChatGPT conversation không bị provider-level deactivate; fencing chỉ rút **Codex executable authority**. Nếu executor bypass helper, alternate checkout/host hoặc filesystem semantics không được chứng minh, dừng; không tuyên bố mutual exclusion ngoài supported boundary.
- Ignored `tmp/` state survives ordinary process restart only while checkout/volume remains; deletion, different checkout/host, volume failure or power loss are not covered by current guarantee. Missing/ambiguous state blocks; no full old-chat replay, no manual lock deletion or budget reset. Timestamps and helper PID are diagnostic, not staleness or approval authority.
- Exact conversation ID/title and bounded references in local records are sensitive metadata, though not transcript content. Only approved local record writes and selected browser targets are in scope. Credentials, tokens, cookies, session persistence, private transcript export, unrelated personal content, auth/provider control, destructive authority, distributed coordination or new executable wire syntax require `NEEDS_REVIEW`.

## Migration Plan

Sau Human Design approval **và** Sensitive Design Gate approval riêng: tạo Tasks/TIC với allowlist ba tracked owners D2, ignored local record/journal/handoff/lock paths và change/review/QA evidence. Implement helper and explicit fail-closed checks, verify NTFS handle lifetime/replacement/crash behavior, Human cập nhật đúng từng live conversation, rồi live verification và federated Browser QA. Formal VERIFY/Gate 3 theo workflow sau đó; Browser QA v1 vẫn độc lập. Rollback federation bằng fencing/terminal state; không bật v1 giữa một run hoặc dùng v1 để giải cứu uncertain delivery/execution. Human xử lý live conversation context riêng. Không có bước migration nào được thực hiện bởi Design này.

## Open Questions

Những lựa chọn cơ bản về owner, v1 grammar, lock API, record/handoff fields, epoch, linearization, crash disposition và QA assertion nằm ở Design này. Tasks/TIC phải chứng minh khả năng implement trên local NTFS và không được nới boundary khi test thất bại. Human còn phải quyết định có chấp nhận single-host/one-checkout, local conversation metadata, fail-closed loss/recovery và rủi ro không có distributed guarantee hay không. Nếu không chấp nhận, dừng trước Tasks/TIC và sửa Design qua review; không suy approval từ việc file này tồn tại.
