The review of the existing controls also includes these exact interactions:

- A cross-lineage, mutable, stale or hash-mismatched material-equivalence reference cannot classify a new bucket. A new stage, evaluator label, run, tower, path or artifact hash is never semantic proof of distinct work. The reviewed classification and its separately attributable Human or authoritative-workflow decision must bind the exact context, lineage and related bucket; otherwise execution stops `NEEDS_REVIEW`.
- One Human interaction proposing both a maximum increase and a freeze transition must produce two separately attributable, exact-scope decision items. Each has its own deterministic identity and replay check. Neither decision implies the other, and an unsupported schema or conflicting prior maximum/active-freeze identity blocks both affected transitions.
- An APPLY after `COMMAND_ACCEPTED` cannot erase the accepted intent or its eventual attributable outcome. It blocks new affected dispatch; `PENDING_COMMAND_ID` and `EXECUTION_UNCERTAIN` retain their at-most-once protection. A lost or uncertain outcome is not redispatched because a freeze is applied or lifted.
- Fresh `RUN_ID`, activation epoch, restart, Page-to-Global escalation and Page/Global instance rotation must carry classification provenance, counters, maxima, exception history, freeze transition history and derived ACTIVE state, pending identity, blocker ancestry and `EVIDENCE_STOP_STATE` together. A missing or conflicting component yields zero executable authority.
- LIFT leaves `APPROVED_MAXIMUM`, `ATTEMPTS_USED` and `EXECUTION_GENERATIONS_USED` unchanged. It cannot clear evidence-stop, unsafe retry, missing mandatory evidence, no-useful-progress stop, pending uncertainty or any independent authorization blocker.
