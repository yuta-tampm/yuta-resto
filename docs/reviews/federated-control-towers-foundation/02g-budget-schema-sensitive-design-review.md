The proposed schema has no second evaluator counter: `EVALUATOR_GENERATIONS` and `EXECUTION_GENERATIONS` denote the same count of actual materially equivalent evaluator executions. Rejected preflight, malformed/stale/duplicate/replayed/unauthorized commands, Human Gate rounds, ordinary read-only diagnosis, timeout, `BLOCKED`, `STOP` and `DONE` do not themselves consume a generation. A read-only action that actually runs the equivalent evaluator does consume one. Counts and their evidence remain independent of an unproven outcome, but pending/uncertainty blocks further dependent execution.
