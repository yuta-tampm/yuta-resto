## Purpose and bounded scope

Veille & conformité is the future Backoffice capability that combines a
digital administrative/compliance dossier with targeted regulatory monitoring
relevant to restaurant activity. It is intended to help a restaurant organize
available information, understand what may require attention, and prepare for
a control without claiming that YUTA establishes current law, applicability,
evidence sufficiency, or legal compliance.

The confirmed high-level Product questions are:

1. Is the available dossier ready if a control occurs tomorrow?
2. Does a new rule concern the establishment, and may action be required?

These questions define Product direction. They do not approve an applicability
engine, legal interpretation, requirement/control/evidence model, or readiness
guarantee.

This home is canonical repository knowledge for the exact migrated Veille &
conformité scope. The Veille & conformité Page Chat is `LEGACY EVIDENCE ONLY`
for this exact scope and remains available for historical or forensic lookup.
